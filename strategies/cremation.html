Keep it simple, still protect your family

Funeral not in your future? Looking to keep things minimal but still protect the people you love? Cremation coverage is an affordable, thoughtful way to do exactly that.

Book Your Free Consultation

Not everyone wants a traditional funeral — and that's perfectly okay. If you'd rather keep things simple, cremation coverage lets you plan for your final wishes affordably, while still making sure your family isn't left holding the bill.

It works like a streamlined final expense policy, sized for cremation and related costs. The benefit goes directly to the person you choose, so they can carry out your wishes without scrambling for money or compromising on what you wanted.

Why it's a smart choice

  • Affordable by design. Because it's built for a smaller, simpler arrangement, premiums are easy on the budget.
  • Your wishes, honored. Money is set aside specifically for cremation, so there's no confusion or guesswork later.
  • No burden on your family. They won't have to cover the cost out of pocket during an already hard time.
  • Simple to qualify. Coverage is typically easy to get, often with no medical exam.
Thoughtful and practical: a small, caring step that takes one more worry off your family's plate.
