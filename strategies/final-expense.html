Cover your final wishes, not leave the bill

Saying goodbye is hard enough. Final expense insurance makes sure your family doesn't also inherit the cost of your funeral and final arrangements.

Book Your Free Consultation

A funeral can easily run into the thousands of dollars, and it tends to arrive at the worst possible moment — when your family is grieving and least prepared for a surprise bill. Final expense insurance is built to take that worry off the table completely.

It's a smaller, whole-life policy designed specifically to cover funeral costs, burial or cremation, medical bills, and other end-of-life expenses. The benefit goes straight to the people you choose, so they can handle everything without dipping into savings or passing the hat.

What makes it reassuring

  • Coverage for life. The policy never expires as long as premiums are paid — it won't run out on you in your later years.
  • Locked-in pricing. Your premium is set and will never increase, and your coverage will never decrease.
  • Easy to qualify. These policies are designed for simple approval, often with just a few health questions and no medical exam.
  • For all ages. Coverage is available across a wide range of ages — it's never too early or too late to plan.
Why it matters: when the time comes, your family won't be making hard financial decisions through tears. Everything is already handled, the way you wanted it.
